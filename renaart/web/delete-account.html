RenaArt Account and Data Deletion

Last updated: 15 April 2026

This page explains how users can request deletion of their RenaArt account and associated data.

How to request account deletion (in-app)

  1. Open the RenaArt app and sign in to your account.
  2. Go to Profile.
  3. Tap Delete Account.
  4. Confirm deletion.

Alternative request method (email)

If you cannot access the app, send a deletion request to: 6631503124@lamduan.mfu.ac.th

What data is deleted

  • RenaArt account profile data (for example nickname, username, and email record linked to your account).
  • Favorites synced to cloud for your account.
  • Issue reports submitted from your account.

What data may remain

  • Technical logs and backup copies may remain for up to 30 days before permanent deletion.
  • Data retained only where required by law or security obligations.

Guest mode note

If you used Guest mode without creating an account, you can remove local app data by uninstalling the app or clearing app data on your device.
